# Metric definitions

## Win rate
Win rate = Won deals divided by closed deals (Won plus Lost). Open deals (Prospecting, Engaging) are excluded from the denominator.

## Sales cycle length
Days between engage_date and close_date for closed deals. Deals with close_date before engage_date are invalid and must be excluded.

## Pipeline value
Open deals are valued at the product's sales_price because close_value is empty until a deal is won.
