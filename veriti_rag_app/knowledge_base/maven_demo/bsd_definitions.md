# Business Success Drivers

## revenue_reporting
Reliable reporting of won revenue. Requires sales_pipeline.close_value, deal_stage and close_date. Depends on rule DRC-COMP-002.

## pipeline_visibility
Seeing every open deal with its owner, account and stage. Requires accounts, sales_teams and sales_pipeline to join cleanly. Depends on rules DRC-INTG-001 and DRC-VALD-002.

## sales_cycle_analysis
Measuring how long deals take from engage_date to close_date. Depends on rule DRC-CONS-001.
